8. Your questions, answered

Is a game or simulation setting too unserious?
A game is, for this purpose. A simulation is not, when it is the instrument engineers already use and its error is stated. Your contact's "fun project or serious?" followed a game clip with no number in it. The real risk is the opposite one: the most serious physics check in this week's sample got 383,000 views and 185 likes because nothing in it looked wrong. A wind tunnel with a person in it avoids both.
Is demoing work that spans several model versions a good approach, and how should you describe it?
Yes, if each part is described as what it is. The body tracker was built with earlier Fable versions between June and August. Everything on this page was built by Fable 5.2 in one night. And in the demo Fable 5.2 is the agent that calls the check: "Fable designs, SoarLabs checks." A post on progress across versions is worth doing as a follow-up a few days later.
Does "Fable designs, SoarLabs checks" hold up?
Yes. The launch page, read tonight, reports 83.5 on BenchCAD 2.1 (Opus 5.5: 65.3) and 64.9% on RoboDojo Sim (Opus 5.5: 39.4%), and describes Fable designing three circuit boards that worked. It describes no physical simulation of any design, and neither did any post I found.
Can the 700-muscle model be done well, or is it a no?
A no for this demo. Section 4.
Is improving the water simulation in Tidewater worth doing for this?
No. It is another developer's water and you like it as it is, a better ocean enters the week's most crowded lane, and your learned water models replay one fixed scene. This is a judgement from the assets and the feed; I ran no experiment.
Should the viral demo be separate from BodyArcade?
Yes: its own page and name, built on BodyArcade's tracker and saying so. BodyArcade is a game platform; this is the SoarLabs product. The tracker's habit of predicting a hidden limb for a third of a second and then giving up is the same idea at small scale, and the demo should use it: when the camera cannot see her, the doubt should rise.
How do you link what your investor contact asked for to the viral demo?
One engine, two doors, as in section 3: the reply to them is the agent door, the post is the same engine with a person standing in it. Keep their name out of both.
